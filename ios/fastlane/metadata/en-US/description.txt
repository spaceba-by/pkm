PKM Reader brings your personal knowledge base to your pocket. Designed for Obsidian vault users who want mobile access to their notes with AI-powered organization.

SMART CLASSIFICATION
Your documents are automatically classified into five categories -- meetings, ideas, references, journals, and projects -- using AI analysis. Filter and browse by classification to find what you need quickly.

AI-GENERATED INSIGHTS
Receive daily summaries of your vault activity and weekly analytical reports. Stay on top of your knowledge base without reading every document.

POWERFUL SEARCH
Full-text search across all your documents with instant results. Find any note by title, content, or path.

TAG BROWSING
Explore your knowledge base through tags. See tag counts at a glance and drill down into documents sharing the same tag.

OFFLINE SUPPORT
Documents are cached locally so you can read them without a network connection. An offline indicator lets you know when you are working from cache.

SECURE BY DESIGN
Authentication is handled through AWS Cognito. Your data stays private and is transmitted securely.

PKM Reader connects to your existing PKM backend powered by AWS. Documents sync automatically from your Obsidian vault via S3, with AI processing handled by Amazon Bedrock.
